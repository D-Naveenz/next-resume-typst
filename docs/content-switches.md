# Content Switches

This document records how Naveen's resume content should shift for different
target roles. It is a human-readable content strategy first; it is not a Typst
runtime switching feature yet.

Use these switches when tailoring the resume, reviewing future branch merges, or
deciding which experience bullets should move up, compress, or drop.

## software-engineering

Current active mode. This version targets software engineering roles while
keeping business operations experience visible enough to explain maturity,
domain understanding, and production judgment.

Entry priority order:

1. AFISOL (Pvt.) Ltd.
2. Keells - Jaykay Marketing Services (Pvt) Ltd.
3. App 360
4. SimCentric Technologies

Strengthen:

- AFISOL production systems work across PMS, HRMS, CMMS, and resource
  monitoring.
- Ownership of Modbus/BACnet resource-monitoring development and the move from
  R&D into production delivery.
- Engineering depth in C#, ASP.NET, Next.js, React, Python, SQL Server,
  Protobuf, and industrial protocols.
- Dhara Toolchain and NextResume as current technical project anchors.

Compress or move lower:

- Keells cashiering/admin details unless they support systems thinking,
  reporting, audits, inventory accuracy, or cross-team coordination.
- Older internships once AFISOL and Keells are strong enough to carry the
  experience section.
- Community entries such as Nexus Mods and Leo Club if the project section gets
  too dense.

Projects to include:

- Dhara Toolchain.
- NextResume.
- Nexus Mods Community and Leo Club only as compact supporting entries when
  layout allows.

Skills to surface:

- C#, Rust, Python, JavaScript/TypeScript.
- .NET, ASP.NET, Next.js, React, WinUI 3.
- Modbus, BACnet, SQL Server, DHBIN, MessagePack, Protobuf, SQLite.
- SAP, Inventory Control, Reporting, Audits, and Process Coordination as
  supporting business systems signals.

Keells note:

Keells must not be treated as filler. In software-engineering mode, it should be
positioned as practical business operations experience that strengthens systems
design judgment, ERP/business-systems empathy, documentation discipline, and
production accountability.

1. AFISOL (Pvt.) Ltd.
2. Keells - Jaykay Marketing Services (Pvt) Ltd.
3. App 360
4. SimCentric Technologies

Strengthen:
